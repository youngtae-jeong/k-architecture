A Study on the Revitalization of Effective Spaces at Bupyeong Hyanggyo

Exploring modern utilization methods for neglected idle spaces within the city, specifically for 'Hyanggyo,' a traditional cultural heritage.

Although Bupyeong Hyanggyo is located in an urban residential area and offers excellent accessibility, it was a space that was difficult for citizens to visit in their daily lives due to limited use for specific events and a sense of psychological distance. Accordingly, this study proposed introducing the concept of a 'Pop-up Space' to Bupyeong Hyanggyo to maximize spatial flexibility and variability. Through this, it presented a creative architectural alternative that goes beyond simply preserving the traditional space to reconfiguring it into a 'life-oriented community and cultural/experience hub' where local residents can participate and interact.
